Five very different things, one shape

A CFD run. A post-processor. A GP fit. An acquisition step. An LLM RAG answer. Each one becomes a flat row — inputs on the left, outputs on the right, with the actor and tool attached. The DAG is what you get when you connect rows by their shared artefacts.
